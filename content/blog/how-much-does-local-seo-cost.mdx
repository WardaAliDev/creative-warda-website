Local SEO for a small business typically costs between 400 and 2,500 pounds a month, depending on how competitive your city and service are and how much work is included. There is no single flat industry rate, but there are clear tiers most agencies fall into.

## What determines the price

Three things move the price more than anything else: how competitive your city is, how competitive your specific service is within that city, and how much of the work is being done from scratch versus refined from an existing foundation. A plumber in a mid-sized town pays less than an electrician in a major city chasing the same three keywords as fifteen other companies.

## The three common pricing tiers

**Basic, 300 to 800 pounds a month.** Covers Google Business Profile management, basic on-page fixes and citation cleanup. Good for a business in a smaller market with limited competition.

**Growth, 1,000 to 2,500 pounds a month.** Adds ongoing content, link building and a more active review and posting strategy. This is the right tier for most established local service businesses in a competitive city.

**Competitive, 2,500 pounds a month and up.** For businesses fighting for rankings in a major city against well-funded competitors, usually involving heavier content production and a dedicated link building budget.

## How to tell if a quote is fair

Ask exactly what is included each month. A number with no explanation of deliverables is a warning sign. A fair quote should list specific work: how many pieces of content, how many links targeted, what citation and review work is included, and what reporting you receive.

## Is it worth it compared to paid ads

Paid ads bring calls in immediately and stop the moment you stop paying. Local SEO takes longer to build, usually 3 to 6 months for meaningful movement, but keeps generating calls without an ongoing cost per click once it is working. Most businesses benefit from running both together rather than choosing one over the other permanently.
