Local Services Ads and Google Ads solve overlapping but different problems, and most local service businesses eventually want both. If you can only start with one, the right choice depends on your budget and how established your business already is.

## The core difference

Google Ads charge per click, whether or not that click turns into a customer. Local Services Ads charge per lead, meaning you only pay when someone actually contacts you through the ad. That single difference changes how much risk each option carries for a new or budget-limited business.

## Why Local Services Ads are often the better starting point

Because you only pay for actual leads, Local Services Ads carry less downside risk while you are still learning what converts. They also display the Google Guaranteed badge, which builds trust with a customer comparing several options at once. The tradeoff is less control over targeting and ad copy compared to standard Google Ads.

## When Google Ads make more sense first

If you already have a landing page built to convert and a clear sense of which keywords bring in your best customers, Google Ads give you far more control over targeting, messaging and budget pacing. Businesses with an existing marketing foundation typically get more out of Google Ads than a business starting from nothing.

## Running both together

Most mature local service businesses eventually run both, using Local Services Ads for lower-risk lead volume and Google Ads for more precise targeting around specific services or high-value jobs. The two do not cannibalise each other since they typically show up in different positions on the search results page.

## A realistic first budget

For most local service businesses, 300 to 800 pounds a month is enough to test either option properly in a single service area and start understanding what a real cost per lead looks like for your specific business, before committing to a larger budget.
