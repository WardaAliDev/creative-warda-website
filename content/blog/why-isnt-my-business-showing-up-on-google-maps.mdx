A business usually disappears from Google Maps for one of seven reasons, and most of them are fixable within a few days. Work through this list in order.

## 1. Your Google Business Profile is not verified

An unverified profile will not rank, no matter how complete it looks. Check your profile dashboard for a verification notice and complete it if one is showing.

## 2. Your categories do not match how people search

If your primary category is too broad or slightly wrong, Google may not connect your profile to the specific searches your customers use. A general contractor listed only as "contractor" instead of also selecting more specific categories will miss searches that use more specific terms.

## 3. Your business name, address and phone number are inconsistent

If your details differ across your website, your Google profile and directory listings like Yelp or industry directories, Google has less confidence you are a real, stable business at that location. Standardising this information across every listing is one of the highest-impact fixes available.

## 4. Your review count and recency are behind competitors

Google's local ranking factors weigh review volume and how recently reviews were left. A competitor with more recent reviews will often outrank a business with a higher star rating but an older, smaller review base.

## 5. You have not posted or updated your profile recently

An inactive profile signals to Google that a business may not be currently active. Posting updates, adding photos and responding to reviews regularly keeps the signal strong.

## 6. Your service areas are set incorrectly

If you serve a wider area than your listed address suggests, and your service area settings are not configured properly, you can be invisible for searches from nearby towns you actually cover.

## 7. A duplicate or suspended listing is competing with your real one

Duplicate listings, old listings from a previous address, or a suspended profile can all interfere with your visibility. Search your own business name to check for duplicates and address any suspension notices immediately.

Most of these are fixable without technical help, but fixing all seven and then maintaining them every month is the actual work behind consistent local rankings.
